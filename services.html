Services

Everything you need to take an app idea from sketch to the App Store, and keep it running afterward.

Mobile App Development

iOS & Android with React Native and Expo: one codebase, both stores.

We build native-feeling mobile apps for iPhone and Android from a single React Native + Expo codebase, so you get both platforms without paying to build twice. From your first tap-through prototype to a polished, store-ready app, we handle the interface, the logic, and everything in between.

What's Included

iOS & Android, One Codebase

Built once in React Native, shipped to both the App Store and Google Play.

Expo Tooling

Faster builds, over-the-air updates, and a smoother path through app review.

Custom UI

Interfaces designed around how your users actually work, not a generic template.

Testing & QA

Real-device testing before anything reaches the app stores.

Web Apps & PWAs

Fast, installable web applications: dashboards, portals, booking tools, directories.

Not every idea needs a native app on day one. We build web apps and Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) that run in the browser, install to a home screen, and work offline where it matters. A great fit for dashboards, booking tools, member portals, and directories.

What's Included

Installable & Offline-Ready

Add-to-home-screen support with offline caching via Capacitor and PWA tooling.

Built for Speed

Lightweight, fast-loading pages that work well on any connection.

Dashboards & Portals

Admin dashboards, customer portals, and internal tools built to your workflow.

Directories & Booking Tools

Searchable listings and booking flows, built for real-world use.

MVP Development

Scoping your idea down to a launchable first version, built in weeks.

Most ideas are bigger than they need to be for a first launch. We work with you to scope down to the smallest version that proves the idea works, then build it fast, so you're gathering real feedback instead of guessing.

What's Included

Scope & Feature Planning

We help cut the feature list down to what your first users actually need.

Built in Weeks

A working, testable version fast, not months of planning before anything ships.

Backends & Data

Firebase authentication, Firestore databases, storage, and notifications.

Every app needs somewhere to keep its data safe and its users signed in. We build on Firebase: authentication, Firestore databases, file storage, and push notifications, so your app has a solid, scalable backend without you needing to manage servers.

What's Included

Authentication

Secure sign-up and login, including social sign-in options.

Firestore Databases

Real-time data that updates instantly across every device.

Storage

Photos, documents, and files stored and served reliably.

Push Notifications

Keep users in the loop with timely, relevant alerts.

App Store Deployment

App Store Connect, Google Play, signing, review, and release management.

Getting an app approved and live is its own maze of certificates, provisioning profiles, and review guidelines. We handle App Store Connect and Google Play Console setup, code signing, submission, and the back-and-forth with app review, so your launch doesn't stall on paperwork.

What's Included

App Store Connect Setup

Listings, screenshots, and metadata prepared and submitted correctly the first time.

Google Play Console Setup

Signing, release tracks, and store listings handled end to end.

Review Management

We track submissions and respond quickly if reviewers ask for changes.

Maintenance & Updates

Bug fixes, OS updates, new features, and store compliance.

Launch is the beginning, not the finish line. We keep your app running as iOS and Android evolve: fixing bugs, shipping new features, and keeping you compliant with ever-changing store policies.

What's Included

Bug Fixes

Issues found and fixed quickly, before they affect more users.

OS & Store Compliance Updates

Staying current as Apple and Google change their requirements.

New Features

Your app keeps growing after launch, not gathering dust.
